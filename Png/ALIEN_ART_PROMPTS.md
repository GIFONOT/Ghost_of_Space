# Пришельцы и фон

Созданы встроенным ImageGen. Обычные враги — живые существа, боссы — монстры на боевых платформах. При загрузке используются прежние ширины и общая пиксельная сетка без сглаживания.

## enemy-raider.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production enemy sprite for Ghost of Space. One living alien MONSTER: a teal turquoise alien beetle with oval segmented chitin shell, six small jointed legs, red eyes and downward mandibles. This is a biological creature floating in space, NOT a spaceship, NOT a robot, NO cockpit, NO guns, NO engines, NO metal. Unified 16-bit arcade pixel art across all monsters: coarse logical 32x32 pixel grid, chunky square pixel clusters, thick dark navy outline, limited 10-color palette, 3 flat shades per surface, clean readable silhouette, glowing tiny biological highlights. Exact orthographic TOP DOWN, head and attacking appendages pointing toward BOTTOM, entire creature visible centered with transparent padding. Symmetric readable body. Genuine transparent background. No cast shadow, no scene, no stars, no text, no UI, no blur, no gradients, no soft glow, no realistic detail. Single sprite only.
