## boss-warden.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: transparent boss sprite for Ghost of Space. Edit the reference boss conservatively into an alien MONSTER using a warship. Preserve the broad crimson and gunmetal shoulder armor, two enormous downward railgun barrels and amber center. Replace just the central reactor/prow area with a clearly living red chitin alien crab torso, a large amber biological eye, small mandibles facing down, and two organic claws gripping the railgun mounts. Some red chitin overlaps the shoulder armor. Keep overall silhouette, palette, weapon placement, orthographic top-down view facing DOWN, dimensions and composition of reference. The creature should be unmistakably visible at game size but mechanical platform stays mostly intact. Match unified coarse 16-bit arcade pixel art: hard square pixel clusters on logical 48-pixel-wide grid, dark navy outlines, limited flat colors, chunky shading. Entire single boss inside frame with padding. Actual transparent background, remove any exterior glow/shadow. No text, no UI, no environment, no extra sprites, no blur.
